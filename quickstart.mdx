Set up your first Paperzilla project and choose how to read the papers it finds.

## Before you start

Paperzilla starts project setup in a guided chat on the public site. You describe the research topic you want to track, then Paperzilla proposes categories and keywords before you save the project.

- You can start without an account.
- Have a short description of your research interest ready.
- If Paperzilla asks you to verify your email, have your inbox handy.

## Step 1: create a project from chat

1. Open [paperzilla.ai](https://paperzilla.ai).
2. Click **Create my research feed**.
3. Describe the research topic you want to track.
4. Review the proposal:
   - interest description
   - keywords Paperzilla will boost
   - keywords Paperzilla will skip
   - source categories
5. Click **Create project**.

PubMed categories are available during your one-time preview and remain available in Starter, Plus, and Pro. If your preview has ended without a trial or subscription, PubMed appears locked and its categories are not included in a new proposal.

## Step 2: confirm your email {#step-2-save-project-delivery}

If Paperzilla asks for your name and email, enter them and request a verification code. Enter the code from your inbox to finish creating the project. Use the same email if you already have an account.

## Step 3: open and manage your project

Paperzilla opens your project. If it’s preparing your first papers, check the progress there and return when your feed is ready.

From the project page, you can:

- Open **Paper Feed** to review matching papers
- Open **Project Settings**
- Click **Edit Project** to change categories, interests, and keywords
- Click **Edit Email Delivery** to choose daily, weekly, or paused delivery when available on your plan
- Click **Copy RSS/Atom URL**
- Open **Paper Digests**
- Open **View Summaries** after summaries exist

## Step 4: choose how you read

- **In app**: [Paper Feed guide](/guides/feed-in-app)
- **RSS/Atom**: [feeds guide](/guides/feeds)
- **CLI**: [CLI guide](/guides/cli)
- **MCP**: [MCP guide](/guides/mcp)
- **Email**: [email digests guide](/guides/email-digests)

If you connected Paperzilla to an AI agent, return to that chat after creating your project. [Ask for your first research brief](/guides/ai-agent-briefs)

## Step 5: refine relevance over time

As papers arrive, improve precision by editing:

- interest text
- include keywords
- exclude keywords
- **Include adjacent papers**

You can also rate papers in the feed as **Useful**, **Very useful**, or **Not for me**.

Use [project management](/guides/projects) for details.
