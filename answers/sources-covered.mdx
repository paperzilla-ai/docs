Paperzilla monitors preprints and indexed research literature from:

- arXiv
- medRxiv
- bioRxiv
- ChemRxiv
- ChinaXiv
- IACR ePrint
- PubMed

Source badges appear on paper cards so you can see origin at a glance.

PubMed is included in Starter, Plus, and Pro. It covers new PubMed-indexed biomedical and clinical literature after you add PubMed categories to a project. Paperzilla does not mirror the full PubMed archive.

PubMed records can be available before an abstract or full text is public. Paperzilla always links to the PubMed record. A PDF action appears only when an open-access PDF URL is available.

IACR ePrint is included in all plans. Choose its cryptography categories in a project to receive newly discovered papers. Paper references use the IACR identifier, such as **IACR ePrint 2026/1234**.
