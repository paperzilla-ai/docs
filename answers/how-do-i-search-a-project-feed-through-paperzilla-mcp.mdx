Use `projects_list` to choose an owned project, then call `feed_search` with `project_id` and `query`. For example: `{"project_id":"your-project-uuid","query":"retrieval augmented generation","limit":20}`. Results are ranked and paginated. Use `next_offset` to continue. The scope is `projects:read`. See the [reference](/api-reference/mcp).
