1. Go to [paperzilla.ai](https://paperzilla.ai).
2. Click **Create my research feed**.
3. Describe your research interests in chat.
4. Review categories and keywords.
5. Click **Create project**.
6. If prompted, verify your email to finish setup.

Open the project to read your feed and manage [sharing](/guides/sharing-and-visibility), [RSS/Atom](/guides/feeds), [summaries](/guides/summaries), and [email settings](/guides/email-digests).
